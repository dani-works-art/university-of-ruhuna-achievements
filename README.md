# UNIACHIEVE RUHUNA – Faculty of Engineering, University of Ruhuna

## About the Project

UNIACHIEVE is a web-based platform developed to collect, organise, and showcase the achievements of the Faculty of Engineering, University of Ruhuna.

The platform aims to bring the achievements of students and societies together in one place, making them easily accessible to students, staff members, and visitors. It addresses the difficulty of finding achievement-related information that is scattered across different sources.

The website features Sports & Athletics, Events, and Clubs & Societies sections. It also includes an achievement submission facility, allowing members of the university community to submit achievements for consideration. UNIACHIEVE aims to recognise student accomplishments, encourage participation in university activities, and preserve the faculty's achievements.

## Objectives

- To provide a centralised platform for showcasing the achievements of the Faculty of Engineering.
- To recognise and celebrate the accomplishments of students and societies.
- To encourage student participation in sports, events, and extracurricular activities.
- To maintain a digital record of the faculty's achievements.
- To make achievement-related information easily accessible to students, staff members, and visitors.

  ## Features

- **Sports & Athletics:** Showcases sporting activities and achievements of the faculty.
- **Events:** Highlights university events, competitions, and cultural celebrations.
- **Clubs & Societies:** Presents the activities and achievements of various student clubs and societies.
- **Achievement Submission:** Allows students and university community members to submit achievements for consideration and       publication.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Provides a simple and organised way to explore the faculty's achievements.

  ## Technologies Used

The following technologies and tools were used in developing the UNIACHIEVE website:

- **HTML5:** Used to create the structure and content of the website.
- **CSS3:** Used to design and style the website.
- **Visual Studio Code:** Used as the code editor.
- **GitHub:** Used to store and manage the project files.
- **GitHub Pages:** Used to host and publish the website online.
- **Formspree:** Used to handle achievement submissions through the website form.
